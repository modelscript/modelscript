## 1. Overview & Purpose

The ModelScript project develops an open-core computational modeling, simulation, and multi-domain engineering framework, alongside the Ansatz federated network platform.

We are deeply committed to free and open-source software and open collaboration. ModelScript follows an **Open-Core** licensing model: the core compiler, language parsers, simulation engine, and Community Edition applications (`packages/*`, `languages/*`, and open-core components of `apps/*`) are published under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`)**, while the Enterprise Edition (`ee/`, `@modelscript/ee`) is governed by the proprietary **ModelScript Enterprise License (`LicenseRef-ModelScript-Enterprise`)** and commercial OEM dual-licensing agreements.

Open-source copyright licenses grant broad freedoms to inspect, copy, compile, modify, and redistribute source code. However, **open-source copyright licenses do not grant rights under trademark law** (as explicitly noted in Section 7(e) of the GNU AGPL v3).

This Trademark Policy defines the rules for using our trademarks, logos, and brand assets. Its primary purpose is to **prevent public confusion, misrepresentation, and fraud**, ensuring that users always know whether software, cloud services, or technical resources originate from the official ModelScript project or from an independent third party.

## 3. General Principles

- **No Confusion:** You may not use the Marks in any manner that creates confusion among users or the public as to whether a product, website, service, or organization is official, or is sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with the ModelScript project.
- **No Deception:** You may not use the Marks in connection with deceptive, malicious, misleading, or defamatory content or services.
- **Separation of Code vs. Brand:** While you have the full legal right under AGPL-3.0 to fork, build, and modify the codebase, you do not have the right to use the official brand names or logos to represent your fork.

## 4. Permitted Uses (No Prior Written Permission Needed)

You may use the Marks without prior written permission in the following circumstances, provided you comply with this policy:

### A. Verbatim Distribution of Official Software

You may use the word marks and official logos to distribute unmodified, verbatim copies of official releases, such as:

- Distributing official pre-compiled binaries or container images (`ghcr.io/modelscript/*`).
- Distributing unmodified source archives downloaded directly from official releases.

### B. Nominative Fair Use (Compatibility & Extensions)

You may truthfully and accurately use the word marks to indicate compatibility, interoperability, or relationships with your own software or services, provided that:

- The mark is used referentially, not as a primary brand identifier.
- Your product name does not imply official endorsement or sponsorship.

**Acceptable examples:**

- _"ACME Plugin for ModelScript"_
- _"SimTool: A Modelica editor compatible with ModelScript"_
- _"Python bindings for the msx CLI"_
- _"Federated node connecting to the Ansatz network"_

**Unacceptable examples (Prohibited without prior written authorization):**

- _"ModelScript ACME Edition"_
- _"Official ModelScript Cloud"_
- _"Ansatz Enterprise Suite"_
- _"The ModelScript Company"_

### C. Community, Academic & Educational Discussion

You may freely use the word marks in articles, conference presentations, academic research papers, tutorials, technical books, and blog posts to refer to or review the ModelScript project, provided no official endorsement is implied.

### D. Hyperlinks

You may use the word marks or official project badges/logos on your website solely as hyperlinks directed to `https://modelscript.org`, `https://ansatz.social`, or the official GitHub repository (`https://github.com/modelscript/modelscript`).

## 5. Uses Requiring Prior Written Permission (Prohibited by Default)

The following uses are **strictly prohibited** unless you have obtained explicit, advance written authorization from Mohamad Omar Nachawati:

### A. Modified Forks & Independent SaaS Offerings (The "Fork" Rule)

If you modify the ModelScript or Ansatz software, or if you operate an independent commercial or hosted cloud simulation service based on a fork:

- You **must remove** all official logos, visual identity assets, and proprietary brand styling.
- You **must not** name the service "ModelScript", "Ansatz", or any confusingly similar variant.
- You must choose a distinctive name and brand identity for your distribution or service. You may state truthful compatibility (e.g. _"Powered by open-source ModelScript technology"_).

### B. Domain Names, Social Handles & App Store Identifiers

You may not register, use, or operate:

- Domain names containing "modelscript", "ansatz", or confusingly similar phonetic variants.
- Social media accounts, channels, or organizations named "modelscript" or "ansatz" that create a false impression of being official project channels.
- Desktop, mobile, or cloud app store listings named in a manner that confuses users regarding official origin.

### C. Clone Sites & Mirror Portals

You may not deploy a public website or mirror portal that replicates the visual brand, logos, and header styling of `modelscript.org` or `ansatz.social` in a way that deceives visitors into believing they are interacting with the official flagship platform.

### D. Merchandise & Commercial Branding

You may not manufacture, sell, or distribute physical merchandise (t-shirts, stickers, mugs, hardware) or commercial products featuring the Marks without a written trademark license.
